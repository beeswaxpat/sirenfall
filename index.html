SIRENFALL: a browser civil-defense siren scene

SIRENFALL is a free browser scene: a shelter overlooking a rainy city. Pull the breaker lever to power a civil-defense siren, synthesized live in your browser with Web Audio, not played from a recording. The siren is two tones a minor third apart, 515 Hz and 618 Hz, generated in real time, with a horn that sweeps once every 15 seconds.

Rain and thunder carry on in the scene, with a shutter and a CRT monitor as additional controls. No signup, no install, works in any modern browser.

Built by Beeswaxpat. Source code: github.com/beeswaxpat/sirenfall (MIT).
