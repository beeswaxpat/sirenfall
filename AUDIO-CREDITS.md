# Audio credits

The siren and every console sound (breaker clack, shutter slam, knob ticks, wipe swash) are synthesized in Web Audio. The four weather recordings below are the only audio files.

| File | Source | Author | License | In repo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `audio/rain.mp3` | [Freesound 681623](https://freesound.org/people/Ambient-X/sounds/681623/), softened and looped | Ambient-X | CC0 | Yes |
| `audio/thunder2.mp3` | [Freesound 581124](https://freesound.org/people/Fission9/sounds/581124/) | Fission9 | CC0 | Yes |
| `audio/thunder1.mp3` | [Pixabay 405128](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/distant-thunder-405128/) | CapaholiczSFX | Pixabay Content License | No |
| `audio/thunder3.mp3` | [Pixabay 364468](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/dry-thunder-364468/), trimmed and faded | Dragon-Studio | Pixabay Content License | No |

The Pixabay Content License allows use inside a project but not redistribution of the files on their own, so those two are not in this repo. The live site serves them; a local clone falls back to synthesized noise, or you can download them from the source pages above into `audio/`.
